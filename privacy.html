Beardless privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Beardless shows you what you might look like without a beard. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Photos

When you tap to create an edit, the photos you chose (your current photo and, if you add one, an older clean-shaven photo) are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and on to Google's Gemini API, which creates the edited image and sends it back to your phone.

We do not store your photos or the edited images on our servers. Google processes them to produce the result under its Gemini API terms; as a paid API customer, Google does not use them to train its models. Photos are not used to identify you, and we do not use face recognition.

Images you save or share stay on your phone and wherever you choose to share them.

What we do keep

To count your free try and the edits you have bought, the app creates a random ID for your device. Against that ID we keep your edit balance, a record of each edit (time, chosen look, whether it worked) and the Apple transaction ID for each purchase. We also briefly record your IP address to stop the free try being misused. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or location.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your card details.

Sharing

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. Our service providers are Supabase (hosting, in the EU/UK) and Google (image editing).

Children

Beardless is not intended for children under 13.

Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time. Deleting the app removes the photos from your phone that the app holds. To ask us to delete the records linked to your device ID, contact us at the address below.
