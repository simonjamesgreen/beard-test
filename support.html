Beardless help

Getting the best result

Use a clear, front-facing photo in good light with your whole beard in view. If you have an older photo of yourself clean-shaven, add it: it shows the AI your real jaw and chin, so the result looks much more like you.

Edits and payments

Your first edit is free and carries a small watermark. After that you can buy packs of 10 or 30 edits. There is no subscription and edits never expire. If an edit fails, you are not charged for it.

Edits belong to the phone they were bought on. If a purchase did not appear, open the app again with an internet connection and it will be added automatically.

Refunds

Payments are made through Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
